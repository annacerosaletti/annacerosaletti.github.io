I am interested in Rural Sociology and Technology Transitions, with a specific focus on how rural communities navigate technology transitions in the context of the climate crisis. I am motivated by how policy can be formed to balance rural values, and technological and environmental priorities

I am a recent graduate of Cornell University with a B.S. in Environment and Sustainability. When I am not working away on my computer you can find me outside, mostly cross country skiing, but also running, climbing, backpacking, hammocking, etc.!
